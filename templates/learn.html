🚩 Red flags
  • Brand name in the URL but not the real domain (paypal.com.evil.xyz)
  • Raw IP address or @ in the link
  • Risky TLDs (.xyz, .top, .tk) and many hyphens or subdomains
  • Urgent words: verify, suspend, unlock, login
  • Shortened links that hide the destination
✅ Stay safe
  • Type the official site address yourself instead of clicking links
  • Never share OTP or passwords by link, SMS or email
  • Enable two-factor authentication
